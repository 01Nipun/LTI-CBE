# Diabetes

Diabetes is a chronic disease that occurs when the body cannot effectively use insulin or does not produce enough insulin.

Common symptoms of diabetes include:
- Frequent urination
- Increased thirst
- Increased hunger
- Fatigue
- Blurred vision
- Slow wound healing

Recommended diet for diabetes:
- High fiber foods
- Whole grains
- Vegetables
- Low sugar foods
- Avoid sugary drinks and processed foods

Vegetarian diet options for diabetes:
- Lentils
- Beans
- Tofu
- Brown rice
- Oats
- Green vegetables


# Hypertension

Hypertension is also known as high blood pressure. It increases the risk of heart disease and stroke.

Common symptoms of hypertension include:
- Headaches
- Shortness of breath
- Nosebleeds
- Chest pain
- Dizziness

Diet recommendations for hypertension:
- Reduce salt intake
- Eat fruits and vegetables
- Avoid processed food
- Maintain healthy weight
- Drink plenty of water


# Heart Disease

Heart disease includes conditions affecting the heart such as coronary artery disease.

Common symptoms:
- Chest pain
- Shortness of breath
- Fatigue
- Irregular heartbeat

Treatment options:
- Lifestyle changes
- Medication
- Surgery
- Regular monitoring


# Skin Allergies

Skin allergies occur when the immune system reacts to certain substances.

Common symptoms:
- Redness
- Itching
- Swelling
- Rashes

Treatment options:
- Antihistamine medication
- Avoid allergens
- Moisturizing creams
- Allergy testing


# Hospital Appointment Procedure

To book a doctor appointment:

1. Visit the hospital reception or website.
2. Select the department.
3. Choose an available doctor.
4. Select a time slot.
5. Confirm the appointment.

Patients must carry:
- Government ID
- Previous medical reports
- Insurance details (if available)


# Hospital Policies

General hospital policies include:

- Patients must arrive 15 minutes before appointment time.
- Outside food is not allowed unless medically necessary.
- Mobile phones must be kept silent inside consultation rooms.
- Visitors are limited to one per patient in ICU wards.


# Diet Guidelines

General healthy diet guidelines include:

- Eat balanced meals with proteins, carbohydrates, and healthy fats.
- Avoid excessive sugar and salt.
- Drink at least 2 liters of water daily.
- Include fresh fruits and vegetables.

Vegetarian diet examples:
- Vegetable salads
- Lentil soups
- Whole grain chapati
- Brown rice with vegetables

Non-vegetarian diet examples:
- Grilled chicken
- Fish curry
- Boiled eggs
- Lean meat


# Allergy Information

Patients may have food allergies such as:

- Peanut allergy
- Gluten intolerance
- Dairy allergy
- Shellfish allergy

Patients should inform doctors about allergies before treatment.


# Preventive Healthcare

Preventive healthcare includes:

- Regular health checkups
- Vaccinations
- Healthy diet
- Regular exercise
- Stress management
